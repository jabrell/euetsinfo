# Collection of ETS price data

Carbon price data under the EU ETS is available in **primary allowance auctions** and **secondary spot** and **futures markets**. Auction price data is made publicly available by the **European Energy Exchange** (EEX), which is responsible for administering the EU ETS auctions. The auction process distinguishes between a common auction platform and country-specific auctions for Germany, Poland and Northern Ireland. Based on the publicly available auction calendar, both the common and the country-specific auctions take place at regular intervals, allowing historic carbon price trends to be derived from primary market data covering the period from 2012 to 2026. 
Given that participation in primary auctions is limited to a relatively small number of bidders, we compare auction prices with secondary market prices to assess consistency across markets. Spot price data is retrieved from the EEX, while futures price data is obtained from the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE). Price differentials across primary auctions, spot markets and futures markets are negligible.
